An emergency fund is cash set aside for surprises: a car repair, a medical bill, a gap between jobs. It is what stops a bad week from turning into debt.

## How much is enough

A common target is **three to six months of essential expenses**. If that feels huge, start with one month. Reaching the first milestone matters more than the final number.

## Where to keep it

Somewhere safe, separate and easy to reach: a high-yield savings account is the usual choice. Keeping it apart from your everyday account makes it harder to spend by accident.

## Fund it from leaks, not sacrifice

Most people can free up money without changing how they live. Add up your recurring charges with the calculator below. Cancelling even two forgotten subscriptions and redirecting that amount into savings builds a fund steadily and painlessly.

## Automate it

Set a transfer for the day after payday. What you never see, you never miss.

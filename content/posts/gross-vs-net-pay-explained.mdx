Your **gross pay** is the number in your offer letter. Your **net pay** (take-home) is what actually lands in your account after taxes and deductions.

## Progressive brackets, in plain English

Income tax is not one flat percentage. Each slice of income is taxed at its own rate: the first slice at a low rate, the next at a higher rate, and so on. Your **marginal rate** is the rate on your last dollar; your **effective rate** is total tax divided by total income, and it is always lower.

That is why a raise never makes you take home less overall: only the extra income is taxed at the higher rate.

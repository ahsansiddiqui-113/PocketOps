The 50/30/20 rule is the easiest budget to start with: **50%** of take-home pay for needs, **30%** for wants, and **20%** for savings and debt payoff.

## What counts as what

- **Needs:** rent or mortgage, groceries, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments, transport to work
- **Wants:** dining out, streaming, hobbies, trips, upgrades you could live without
- **Savings:** emergency fund, retirement, investing, and any extra debt payments

## When the rule doesn't fit

In high-cost cities, needs can reach 60% or more. That is fine as a starting point, but it is worth knowing: once needs plus debt pass roughly 55% of take-home pay, you have little room to absorb surprises.

If you carry high-interest debt, shift part of the wants slice into debt payoff until it is gone.

Most people underestimate their subscriptions by a wide margin, because each charge is small and the billing cycles differ: some weekly, some yearly.

## Why they hide

Annual plans renew once, so you forget them. Free trials roll into paid plans. Family plans get split but never cancelled when someone leaves.

## A 10-minute audit

- Scan three months of bank and card statements
- List every recurring charge, with its billing cycle
- Mark each as *use weekly*, *use monthly* or *forgot about it*

Enter everything into the calculator below. It converts weekly, quarterly and yearly charges to a monthly figure so you can compare them fairly, and shows what the money could grow to if invested instead.

## Decide with a simple rule

If you have not used it in 30 days and you would not re-buy it today, cancel it.
